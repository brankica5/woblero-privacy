Privacy Policy — Woblero (Wobli)

Effective date: 8 July 2026
App: Woblero — Wobli ("Wobli", "the app", "we")
Contact: brankica.marinkovic88@gmail.com

Wobli is made for young children (ages 3–7) and their families. Protecting children's privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version: Wobli collects nothing. Wobli sends nothing. Wobli works fully offline. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no microphone, and no recording.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not use the microphone and we do not record audio or video. Wobli responds only to on-screen touch.
  • We do not use the camera or take photos of the child.
  • We do not show advertising of any kind.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not ask for names, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data.
  • We do not create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • We do not include social features, chat, or links that take a child out of the app (any parent links sit behind a parental gate).

What is stored (on the device only)

To remember "your Wobli" between sessions, the app saves a few small settings locally on the device only — for example: Wobli's look/colour, sound on/off, and simple play counters that let Wobli feel familiar. This information never leaves the device, is not personal or identifying, and is removed if you delete the app.

No data leaves the device

Because the app is fully offline for its core experience, no information is sent to us or to any third party. We have no servers that receive your data.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Wobli is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation provisions for children (GDPR-K), Apple's "Made for Kids" requirements, and the Google Play Families policy. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete from our side. Deleting the app removes the local settings described above.

Purchases (if/when offered)

The core experience is free. If optional extras ("packs") are offered in the future, any purchase is a one-time, parent-initiated action placed behind a parental gate, processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive payment details; those are handled by the store under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Wobli requests no sensitive permissions for its core experience. It does not request microphone, camera, location, or contacts access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version with the app. Material changes will be reflected in the store listing.
